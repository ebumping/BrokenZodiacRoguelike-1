B. Design bible (answer to the prompt)
1. Core loop & controls
Players clear procedurally-generated rooms, harvest Motes of Insight (currency), draft Tarot cards or gamble at occult kiosks, and descend until the group wipes or defeats the eldritch Avatar. PC uses WASD + mouse aim/left-click fire, right-click spell, scroll cycle spells—mirrored by dual on-screen sticks and gestures on mobile, matching best-practice twin-stick layouts

5. Weapons, rarity & affixes
6 archetypes (pistols, rifles, shotguns, staves, launchers, melee relics).

Rarity ladder: Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Mythic. Higher tiers unlock affix sockets (1-3).

Affixes pull from ~120 prefixes/suffixes balanced via tier-weighted drop-tables, a best practice in ARPGs 
Reddit
Reddit
. Example—“Howling Staff of the Abyss” rolls +Toxic Cloud on crit & -10 % cast speed.

Mythic weapons inherit an Epithet that rewires core behaviour (e.g., ricochet beams).
