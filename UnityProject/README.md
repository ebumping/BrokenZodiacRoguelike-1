# CODEX OF THE BROKEN ZODIAC

## Version 1.0 Release

An immersive isometric twin-stick roguelike shooter that explores complex player transformation mechanics through innovative zodiac-based sanity systems. The game delivers a unique cosmic horror experience with dynamic, procedurally generated gameplay and sophisticated horror event generation.

![Game Banner](DocumentationImages/game_banner.png)

## Core Features

- **Isometric Twin-Stick Roguelike**: Fast-paced shooting action with procedurally generated levels
- **Zodiac-Based Sanity System**: Players undergo transformations based on their chosen zodiac sign
- **Tarot Card Progression**: Collect and combine cards to create powerful effects and synergies
- **Spell Crafting**: Combine spell components with zodiac resonances for custom abilities
- **Destructible Environments**: Discover secret walls and hidden passages
- **Cosmic Horror Elements**: Face eldritch horrors and reality distortions
- **Multiplayer Support**: 1-4 player cooperative gameplay

## Core Systems

### Sanity System
The central gameplay mechanic that affects perception, triggers transformations, and unlocks abilities. As sanity decreases, players experience visual distortions, hallucinations, and eventually undergo zodiac transformations.

### Tarot Card System
Collect Major and Minor Arcana cards that provide passive and active abilities. Combine cards to create powerful synergies and unlock special effects.

### Spell System
Unlock and upgrade elemental spells, including custom spells created through the Spell Crafting system. Each zodiac sign has unique resonances with specific spell combinations.

### Procedural Generation
Experience unique layouts every playthrough with procedurally generated rooms, encounters, and horror events.
